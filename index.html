Rules that apply to every step
  • Train or fine-tune only on the official Training set. Public general-purpose pretrained models are fine; write each one down with its source.
  • Challenge videos (IMG_0114, IMG_0115): run your method on them and pick court points by hand. Never train on them, never hand-correct boxes, tracks, teams or jerseys.
  • Never download or use other TrackID3x3 annotations, including the public top-view GT of the challenge games.
  • Output rows: frame_id,court_x,court_y,team_id,jersey_number, no header, frames start at 1, every frame kept, centimetres, team A (offense at the start) or B (defense at the start).
What changed on Oct 19 (Days 4–10 rewritten, sprint ends Oct 26)
  • The training videos already show the oblique challenge view, so all eight are validation material. The top-view domain-gap work is gone.
  • TI-HOTA rewards precise positions: credit is about 0.94 at 14 cm, 0.76 at 30 cm, 0.47 at 50 cm, and 0.05 at 100 cm. The strictest threshold (α 0.95) only accepts 13 cm.
  • A wrong team or jersey scores zero even with a perfect position, so identity errors cost the most.
  • GT has all six players in every frame; the tutorial misses ~28%. Outputting six identities per frame is the cheapest big gain (Day 6).
  • Challenge videos are .MP4, Colab is case-sensitive, and the tutorial ran Python 3.13; fixed on Day 4. Run the oracles (Day 5) before spending effort.
  • Two people: (A) detection, positions, output policy · (B) crops, jersey, teams, identity.

Extra steps worth trying
  • Tune every threshold (conf, margin, stitching, vote share, row-emission confidence) with a small search on cached data, e.g. Optuna, scored by fold TI-HOTA.
  • Vote across two recognisers (EasyOCR + PARSeq or your fine-tuned model) per tracklet; disagreements become "unknown".
  • Read jerseys only on the 40–60 best crops per tracklet: faster and less noise.
  • Run the detector at 1920 px only if far-side recall is low; keep 1280 elsewhere.
  • Smooth identity labels over time (HMM/Viterbi) so a label can't flicker between frames.
  • Sanity checks before writing: 3 players per team, speed under ~10 m/s, no duplicate identities.
  • Watch one overlay video per change; numbers hide systematic errors such as a flipped axis.
Training & fine-tuning allowed by the rules
  • Detector fine-tune on Training-set boxes (real, or derived by matching detections to Training GT).
  • Jersey recogniser (PARSeq or a digit classifier) on auto-labelled Training crops.
  • Legibility classifier: label a crop "legible" when the recogniser reads it correctly, then train a small CNN.
  • ReID fine-tune on Training crops grouped by identity (helps stitching).
  • Position-correction regression on matched Training pairs.
  • Final retrain on all eight videos, only if the validation gain was clear; log it.
Not allowed
  • Training, fine-tuning or pseudo-labelling on IMG_0114 / IMG_0115.
  • Hand-correcting boxes, tracks, teams, jerseys or positions on challenge videos (court clicks only).
  • Other TrackID3x3 annotations, including the public top-view GT of the challenge games.
  • Weights trained on the challenge sequences.
