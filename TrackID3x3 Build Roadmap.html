Rules that apply to every step
  • Train or fine-tune only on the official Training set. Public general-purpose pretrained models are fine; write each one down with its source.
  • Challenge videos (IMG_0114, IMG_0115): run your method on them and pick court points by hand. Never train on them, never hand-correct boxes, tracks, teams or jerseys.
  • Never download or use other TrackID3x3 annotations, including the public top-view GT of the challenge games.
  • Output rows: frame_id,court_x,court_y,team_id,jersey_number, no header, frames start at 1, every frame kept, centimetres, team A (offense at the start) or B (defense at the start).
